Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Shorts App is a private tool used only by its developer, Kristoff Red, to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel and read that channel's statistics.

What it accesses

With your permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos (youtube.upload), read your channel and video details (youtube.readonly) and read YouTube Analytics reports for your own videos (yt-analytics.readonly).

How data is stored and shared

All data stays on the developer's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it stored.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
